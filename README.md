### Hi, I'm Ali.

Dissecting complex systems to find the human story inside them is not just my day job; it is a twenty-year baseline. Long before falling completely into the gravitational pull of Data Science and Machine Learning, the core principles of a data professional were already driving my every move. Now, the veil is finally pulled back.

Sitting right at the chaotic intersection where raw user experience meets upstream engineering, waiting for permission to fix broken systems is unthinkable. Influence happens entirely without authority. When support data gets messy, I centralize the noise, structure the diagnostics, and force it into clean, validated insights so developers can actually fix the root cause. When I uncovered fragmented billing data, I migrated six years of history using Python and built a relational database to track it myself.

Stagnation is my absolute Achilles heel. Standing still? Not gonna ship. I treat my own life and professional development like a literal CI/CD pipeline, aggressively building, testing, and deploying the next upgraded version of my skill set. Currently completing a Post-Graduate Program in Data Science with Generative AI at UT Austin McCombs, I do not just study the concepts; I live them. I built an entire multi-agent AI Village with shared memory to manage my project governance, and I am actively standing up automated portfolio ecosystems using Google Cloud Platform, Terraform, and Azure DevOps. Every technical discipline I collect goes into building a single, high-performance engine.

This repository exists to put my miles on the odometer on full display. The ink might be fresh on some of my official certificates, but I have been doing the heavy lifting and systems architecture in the shadows for a long time. Stepping into a data-driven operational insights role is the only logical next step because finding the story inside a mountain of data is exactly what I already do every single day. Now, I am just writing it in code.
