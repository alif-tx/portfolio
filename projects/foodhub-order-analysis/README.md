# FoodHub Analytics: Ungapping the Customer Experience

> 📓 **[View the full notebook, rendered →](https://github.com/alif-tx/portfolio/blob/main/projects/foodhub-order-analysis/FoodHub%20Order%20Analysis%20Files/foodhub_order_analysis.ipynb)**

Raw operational data does not lie. It exposes exactly where a business bleeds efficiency and where it actually prints money. This repository houses a relentless Exploratory Data Analysis executed for FoodHub, a digital aggregator struggling with logistical blind spots. Waiting for stakeholders to guess what is broken is not in my DNA. This project forces the truth to the surface using rigorous statistical validation, proving exactly how to optimize delivery times, capture lost revenue, and ruthlessly scale the customer experience.

### The Methodology & Unique Perspective
Data science is only as powerful as the operational instincts driving it. This analysis was built to influence without authority by making the gaps undeniable.

*   **Refusing to Lose Data:** When a massive 38.8 percent of orders showed up without customer ratings, dropping those rows? Not on my watch! Total data integrity was preserved through calculated mean imputation, keeping the sample size completely intact without skewing the behavioral insights.
*   **Engineered Visuals:** Standard charts do not drive boardroom decisions. Custom visualizations were engineered from scratch using a bespoke Berry and Gold color palette, forcing non-technical audiences to instantly see the logistical bottlenecks and revenue anchors.
*   **Statistical Proof Over Intuition:** Gut feelings do not validate operational changes. When the data hinted at weekday delivery delays, a Welch t-test was deployed to mathematically prove the 5.87-minute gap was a systemic platform failure (p < 0.0001), not just random sampling noise.

### The Impact & Insights
*   **The Revenue Engine:** Segmenting the data uncovered a massive skew in profitability. Premium orders over $20 make up only 29.24 percent of the volume, yet they drive an outsized 59.8 percent of total net revenue.
*   **Logistical Bottlenecks Identified:** Statistically verifying that weekday operations run nearly six minutes slower than weekends hands leadership the exact mathematical proof needed to launch dynamic estimated delivery times and save the user experience.
*   **The Powerhouse Tier:** The analysis exposed extreme market concentration. Out of hundreds of vendors, just five elite restaurants carry 33.4 percent of the entire platform's order volume.
*   **Closing the Feedback Loop:** Identifying the 38.8 percent engagement gap in unrated orders prompted a strategic operational push for automated post-delivery rating prompts to hold restaurants accountable.

### The Result
The department leadership and program management team fully embraced the project, holding its insights and recommendations in high regard and noting that the work significantly exceeded exceptional standards. Proof of what happens when raw operational intuition is backed by Python and ruthless inferential statistics.
